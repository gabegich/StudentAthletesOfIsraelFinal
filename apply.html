What You'll Receive

Train. Study. Both.

You don't trade one ambition for another. You step into a structure built so elite sport and a real university degree can coexist — with the people, time, and money to make both work.

Academic Flexibility

Deadlines, exams, and attendance structured around your competition calendar — not the other way round.

Scholarship Support

Tuition and living support scaled to your level, so finances never decide whether you compete or study.

Dedicated Coordinator

One person who knows your season and your syllabus, clearing obstacles before they become crises.

The Same Degree

Not a watered-down credential. The identical Bar-Ilan degree every other student earns.

Cohort of Peers

A team of fellow elite athletes who understand the double life — and push you through it.

Life After Sport

Mentorship and a network that turn today's discipline into tomorrow's career.

The Process

What Happens Next.

Every application is read by a person, not a filter. Here's exactly how it goes.

1

You Apply

Ten minutes. Tell us who you are, your sport, and your level.

2

We Review — Within 2 Weeks

The SAI team reviews every submission personally.

3

A Conversation

Shortlisted athletes are invited to speak with the team before any decision.

4

Your Decision

A clear answer and, for those accepted, a place in Cohort 2.
