Apply to become a Student Athlete of Israel. The application takes about 10 minutes. We review every submission personally and respond within two weeks.

What You'll Receive

Train. Study. Both.

When you join SAI, you don't trade one ambition for another. You step into a structure built so elite sport and the same degree as every other student can coexist — with the people, time, and money to make both work.

Life After
Sport

Track A · Olympic & Paralympic
For national-team and international competitors
Maximum scholarship, full academic flexibility, dedicated coordinator, extended degree timeline. Built around the demands of an elite competition calendar.
Track B · High Performance
For rising national-level athletes
Partial scholarship, structured academic accommodations, dual-identity coaching. A pathway to Track A as your competitive level rises.
What Happens Next
We review every application within 2 weeks. Shortlisted athletes will be invited for a conversation with the SAI team before a final decision is made. Questions? Email apply@studentathletes.org.il.
